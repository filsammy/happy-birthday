The Girl Behind the Curtain

SCROLL

There was once a girl who belonged to the stage.

Under the lights, she became someone else.

She could make a room full of strangers laugh, cry, or hold their breath.

She could turn a few lines into something people would remember long after the show was over.

And somewhere, there was someone who loved watching her do it.

He had never sat in the audience to watch her perform.

He had only seen glimpses of her through recordings she had shared, little pieces of the world she belonged to.

But even through a screen, he could see it.

There was something different about her there.

He loved the way she disappeared into her characters, as if for a little while the girl he knew stepped aside and someone new took her place.

He found that beautiful.

But he also wondered about the girl behind the curtain.

When the lights turned off.
When the makeup came off.
When the costume was put away.
When the applause was gone and there was no character left to play.

He wondered what she was like then.

Maybe tired.
Maybe laughing about something that happened backstage.

Maybe already thinking about the next thing she had to do.

Or maybe, for a little while, just herself.

That was the girl he wanted to know.

Not the girl beneath the spotlight.
Not because she was an artist,
or because she could make a room fall in love with her.

Just her.

The girl who laughed at little things.
The girl who had bad days.
The girl who got excited about things she loved.
The girl who didn't always have to be beautiful, or confident, or extraordinary.

She could just be a girl who had a long day.

A girl who needed someone to listen.

A girl who deserved love even when there was no performance to love about.

And if she ever wondered whether someone would still be there when the lights went out,
he hoped she would remember this:

Some people fall in love with the characters.

Some people fall in love with the applause.

But he fell in love with the girl who came back to herself after every show.

Because the lights will eventually go out.
The music will eventually stop.
The curtain will eventually fall.

But I will be here — to listen, to stay, to love the girl behind the curtain.

My favorite part has never been the show.

It's the person who comes back to me after the curtain falls.

It was always you.
